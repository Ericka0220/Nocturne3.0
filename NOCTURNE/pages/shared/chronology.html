The Chronology

A developing timeline of significant events, discoveries, expeditions, and changes throughout the NOCTURNE universe.

About the Timeline

The Chronology connects events across departments and collections. Dates may be exact, estimated, disputed, or unknown. Entries should retain their original source references.

The timeline is not complete. Some events have no known date, and some historical accounts contradict one another.

BEFORE THE FORMAL ARCHIVE / DATE UNKNOWN

The Earliest Accounts

Surviving folklore and scattered documents describe unusual places, unidentified beings, and unexplained events. Their relationship to one another remains uncertain.

Multiple expeditions document conflicting routes and unusual observations within a remote forest region.

CURRENT ARCHIVE PERIOD / ONGOING

The Living Archive

New discoveries, corrections, and unresolved questions continue to expand the network. The chronology remains open to revision.

Chronology Standards

  • Exact dates are recorded only when supported by evidence.
  • Estimated dates are marked as estimates.
  • Conflicting timelines are preserved separately.
  • Undated events are not assigned arbitrary positions.
  • Every major event should link to its related records.

History is not a straight line. Sometimes the missing pieces matter most.
