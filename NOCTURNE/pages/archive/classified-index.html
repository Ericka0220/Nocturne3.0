The Classified Index

Record Classifications

VERIFIED

Supported by sufficient evidence within the context of the investigation.

UNCONFIRMED

Reported, but lacking enough evidence for a reliable conclusion.

LEGENDARY

Preserved as folklore or traditional storytelling, without proof of the supernatural claim.

DISPUTED

Evidence or accounts conflict, or the record's authenticity is questioned.

UNRESOLVED

An event or report is documented, but its cause remains unknown.
