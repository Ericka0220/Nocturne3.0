Preservation of History

This collection preserves historical material, documented folklore, oral traditions, and fictional records belonging to the NOCTURNE universe.

Each entry should identify its origin and distinguish surviving historical evidence from later interpretation.

DOCUMENTED FOLKLORE

The Wild Hunt

A tradition found in various European folklore accounts describing a spectral procession, often associated with storms, winter nights, and supernatural riders.

Interpretations vary by region and historical period. This record documents the tradition, not proof of a supernatural procession.

FICTIONAL NOCTURNE RECORD

The Black Ledger of Veyr

A fictional document said to contain the names of researchers who disappeared from the network's earliest surviving records.

Several pages appear to have been removed.

Source Integrity

Real historical sources and folklore should be credited and identified. Invented histories must remain clearly marked as fictional.
