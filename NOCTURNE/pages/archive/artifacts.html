OBJECT COLLECTION · 0102

Artifacts

Recovered objects, unusual materials, and items of uncertain origin.

Collection Guidelines

Artifacts are catalogued according to their known history, reported properties, physical condition, and associated accounts.

Unusual claims are recorded separately from experimentally demonstrated properties.

DISPUTED · ARTIFACT 001

The Glassbound Pendant

Material: Unknown glass-like substance

Origin: Disputed

Condition: Intact

A dark pendant appearing in several unrelated accounts. It is said to reflect unfamiliar surroundings instead of the viewer.

No photograph has been authenticated.

UNCONFIRMED · ARTIFACT 002

The Ashen Compass

Material: Brass and darkened metal

Origin: Unknown

Condition: Unlocated

A compass described in a recovered expedition journal. Its needle reportedly points toward places rather than directions.

The original journal ends halfway through a sentence.

UNRESOLVED · ARTIFACT 003

The Thirteenth Key

Material: Blackened iron

Origin: Unknown

Condition: Missing

A key described in an inventory that lists twelve locked rooms and thirteen keys. No surviving record identifies the final lock.
