UNCONFIRMED · ENTITY 001

The Hollow Stag

Category: Forest Entity

Reported Habitat: Dense woodland

First Account: Unknown

Physical Evidence: Inconclusive

Descriptions refer to a large stag-like figure with unusually dark features. Several accounts describe an absence of sound preceding its appearance.

Field note: Witnesses disagree about the antlers. One account claims they resemble bare tree branches.

LEGENDARY · ENTITY 002

The Lantern Widow

Category: Folklore Figure

Reported Habitat: Roads and forest edges

First Account: Regional oral tradition

Physical Evidence: None established

A figure described in scattered stories as a veiled woman carrying a lantern. Accounts differ on whether she guides travelers or leads them astray.

Margin note: The lantern is always described as lit. No account agrees on what fuels it.

UNRESOLVED · ENTITY 003

The Pale Listener

Category: Unknown

Reported Habitat: Abandoned structures

First Account: Recovered testimony

Physical Evidence: No confirmed sample

An unidentified presence reported by witnesses who describe hearing their own voices repeated from empty rooms.

Do not assume the sound is an echo.

Research Reminder

These are fictional NOCTURNE universe entries. Real-world folklore may inspire individual records, but a legend is not proof that a creature exists.
