OBSERVATORY / TEMPORAL STUDIES / 060

THE HOURS THAT DO NOT BELONG

Temporal Anomalies

Missing time. Repeated moments. Events recorded before they occur.

On the Measurement of Time

Temporal anomalies are fictional phenomena involving inconsistent or unusual sequences of events. They may be associated with particular locations, objects, or Veil disturbances.

Researchers do not assume that every inaccurate clock or conflicting memory indicates a temporal event. Ordinary explanations must be considered first.

TEMPORAL / TA-001 / UNCONFIRMED

The Missing Hour

Reported effect: Observers describe an unexplained gap in their recollection of time spent in a particular forest.

Duration: Accounts vary between forty minutes and several hours.

Location: The Hollowing Woods.

Research note: No independent measurement has confirmed the reported time loss.

TEMPORAL / TA-002 / DISPUTED

The Repeating Corridor

Reported effect: A corridor appears to return travelers to the same doorway despite changes in direction.

Duration: Unknown.

Location: An abandoned estate.

Research note: The building's floor plan contains inconsistencies, but no confirmed repeat event has been recorded.

TEMPORAL / TA-003 / UNRESOLVED

The Premature Letter

Reported effect: A letter describing an event appears in an archive before the event's recorded date.

Duration: Not applicable.

Location: Central Archive.

Research note: The letter's origin, delivery date, and original author remain unknown.

TEMPORAL / TA-004 / LEGENDARY

The Thirteenth Bell

Reported effect: A clock tower is said to strike thirteen times, after which one day disappears from the calendar.

Duration: One missing day, according to folklore.

Location: Unknown.

Research note: No reliable record of the tower has been found.

Research Questions

  • Can the same event be independently timed by separate observers?
  • Does the anomaly affect memory, clocks, or the sequence of events itself?
  • Can a temporal disturbance be tied to a specific location?
  • Do written records change when an anomaly occurs?

If the clock is wrong, check another clock. If every clock is wrong, leave a note.
