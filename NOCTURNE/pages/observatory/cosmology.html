THE SHAPE OF EVERYTHING

Cosmology

Models of existence, the boundaries of the universe, and the places beyond what can be observed.

The Three Layers

NOCTURNE uses a three-layer model to organize its fictional universe. It is a working framework, not a complete explanation of existence.

LAYER / 01

The Known World

The ordinary world of geography, history, cultures, and natural laws. It is the part of existence most inhabitants recognize and understand.

Research focus: Historical records, geographical mapping, natural phenomena, and the documented origins of unusual stories.

LAYER / 02

The Veil

A boundary or condition through which unusual phenomena may occur. It is associated with unstable locations, distorted perception, unusual passage, and unexplained interactions.

Research focus: Veil regions, spatial anomalies, unusual environments, and points of contact.

LAYER / 03

The Beyond

A designation for places, entities, and phenomena that appear to exist outside the known structure of the world.

Research focus: Unknown regions, unidentified entities, incomplete cosmological models, and accounts that cannot be independently verified.

Unresolved Cosmological Questions

  • Is the Veil a place, a boundary, a condition, or something else?
  • Does the Beyond have a consistent structure?
  • Are there layers of existence that NOCTURNE has not yet discovered?
  • Can the Known World be observed from beyond the Veil?
  • Are our models describing reality, or merely the limits of our instruments?

The map of existence is not the same thing as existence itself.
