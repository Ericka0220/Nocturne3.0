Purpose of the Observatory

The Observatory documents celestial phenomena, unusual astronomical patterns, temporal disturbances, and reports of objects that do not appear in established star catalogues.

Researchers compare observations across locations and time periods, looking for patterns that may connect the Known World to the Veil and the Beyond.

Not every strange light is an anomaly. Not every anomaly belongs to the sky.
