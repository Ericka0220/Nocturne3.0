Researcher's note: Never mistake an explanation for an answer.

On the Nature of Magic

Within the NOCTURNE universe, magic describes a collection of unusual interactions between living things, objects, places, and the Veil. Its workings are not fully understood. Different disciplines appear to follow different rules, and no single theory explains every recorded occurrence.

This section gathers known systems, experimental practices, symbolic languages, and accounts that remain disputed.

Principles of Study

  • Every effect must have a documented source, even if that source is unknown.
  • Costs and consequences must never be omitted from a record.
  • Repeated accounts do not automatically establish truth.
  • Experimental theories must remain separate from established findings.
  • No magical system is assumed to be limitless.

The unknown is not permission to invent certainty.
