GRIMOIRE / RELIC COLLECTION / 054

OBJECTS WITH UNUSUAL HISTORIES

Relics

Objects whose recorded histories suggest an association with unusual phenomena.

Not every old object is a relic. Some are ordinary objects surrounded by extraordinary stories. Others display effects that remain unexplained. Each item must be studied independently.

RELIC / REL-001 / UNCONFIRMED

The Mourning Compass

Appearance: A tarnished brass compass with a cracked glass face.

Origin: Allegedly recovered from a ship that does not appear on any surviving registry.

Reported property: The needle points toward a person the holder has recently lost, rather than north.

Limitations: No controlled observation has confirmed the effect.

Storage: Archive Cabinet R-04.

RELIC / REL-002 / DISPUTED

The Glass Thimble

Appearance: A small translucent thimble with a faint silver seam.

Origin: Attributed to an unnamed craftsperson in three conflicting accounts.

Reported property: Reflections appear to move a fraction of a second later than the object reflected.

Limitations: The effect has only been described in a single handwritten report.

Storage: Awaiting examination.

RELIC / REL-003 / UNRESOLVED

The Winter Key

Appearance: An iron key that remains cold regardless of its surroundings.

Origin: Found beneath the floorboards of a house absent from local property records.

Reported property: A faint frost pattern appears on nearby glass when the key is moved.

Limitations: No matching lock or door has been identified.

Storage: Preservation Vault B.

Relic Handling Record

Every relic entry should include photographs or illustrations, physical description, provenance, known owners, observed effects, examination history, storage location, and current status.

The story of an object may be older than the object itself.
