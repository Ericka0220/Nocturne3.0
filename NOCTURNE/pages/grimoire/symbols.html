MARKS LEFT BEHIND

Symbols

An index of fictional glyphs, seals, inscriptions, and recurring visual patterns.

Symbols appear throughout the records of the Veil. Some are found carved into objects or structures. Others appear in manuscripts, dreams, and places where no writing should exist.

Researchers must distinguish between a symbol's documented appearance and any interpretation assigned to it.

GLYPH / G-001

The Open Eye

[ Illustration placeholder: an eye inside an incomplete circle ]

Common interpretation: Observation, awareness, or the act of witnessing.

Recorded locations: Two archive fragments and one abandoned observatory.

Status: Meaning unconfirmed.

GLYPH / G-002

The Broken Crown

[ Illustration placeholder: a crown with one missing point ]

Common interpretation: Lost authority, a divided order, or an incomplete cycle.

Recorded locations: A damaged stone tablet and a disputed royal seal.

Status: Related examples may be unrelated designs.

GLYPH / G-003

The Hollow Star

[ Illustration placeholder: a five-pointed star with an empty center ]

Common interpretation: Absence, distance, or an unreachable point.

Recorded locations: Several maps associated with unexplained celestial events.

Status: Under investigation.

GLYPH / G-004

The Returning Thread

[ Illustration placeholder: a line looping back through itself ]

Common interpretation: Repetition, return, or an interrupted passage.

Recorded locations: A collection of handwritten expedition notes.

Status: Unclassified.

Reading the Marks

Glyphs are catalogued by shape, material, location, surrounding marks, and source record. Interpretations are stored separately so that new evidence can change an old reading without erasing the original observation.

Do not force a meaning onto a mark simply because it resembles something familiar.
