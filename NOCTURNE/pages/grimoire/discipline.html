GRIMOIRE / DISCIPLINES / 051

BRANCHES OF THE UNUSUAL

Disciplines

A field guide to the practices and patterns grouped under magical study.

These disciplines are research categories used by NOCTURNE. They are not necessarily separate forces. Some overlap, while others may be different interpretations of the same phenomenon.

DISCIPLINE / 001

Veilcraft

The study of effects associated with boundaries between the Known World, the Veil, and the Beyond. Reports include distortions of distance, perception, and passage.

Known limitation: Effects appear highly dependent on location and environmental conditions.

DISCIPLINE / 002

Resonance

The study of unusual responses to sound, rhythm, vibration, and repeated patterns.

Known limitation: Similar sounds do not consistently produce similar results.

DISCIPLINE / 003

Vital Studies

Research into phenomena associated with living systems, growth, decay, and biological anomalies.

Known limitation: Observed effects are unpredictable and cannot be assumed to be transferable between organisms.

DISCIPLINE / 004

Symbolic Arts

The examination of marks, inscriptions, diagrams, and repeating visual structures associated with unusual effects.

Known limitation: A symbol's appearance alone does not establish its function.

DISCIPLINE / 005

Chronomancy

The fictional study of time-related anomalies, including inconsistent sequences, missing intervals, and repeated moments.

Known limitation: No reliable method of controlling time has been established.

DISCIPLINE / 006

Oneirology

The study of unusual connections between dreams, memory, and phenomena reported beyond ordinary waking experience.

Known limitation: Memory and dream accounts are subjective and require careful corroboration.

DISCIPLINE / 007

Relic Studies

The examination of objects believed to preserve, conduct, or distort unusual effects.

Known limitation: An object's history may be incomplete or deliberately fabricated.

A discipline is a question asked repeatedly, not an answer carved in stone.
