GRIMOIRE / HYPOTHESES / 055

IDEAS STILL WAITING FOR EVIDENCE

Theories

Not every explanation survives investigation. These are the ideas that have not yet been settled.

Why Theories Matter

NOCTURNE preserves theories even when they are incomplete or later disproven. A discarded explanation may still reveal how a previous researcher understood the evidence.

Each theory is assigned a status that reflects its current support, not its popularity.

THEORY / TH-001 / UNDER REVIEW

The Memory Imprint Hypothesis

Proposal: Certain locations retain impressions of emotionally significant events, which may be experienced by later visitors as sounds, images, or sensations.

Supporting accounts: Repeated reports of similar experiences in the same locations.

Challenges: Reports may be influenced by expectation, local legends, and shared storytelling.

Next question: Can similar accounts be documented independently?

THEORY / TH-002 / DISPUTED

The Living Map Theory

Proposal: Certain maps connected to the Veil may change because the places they describe are not geographically stable.

Supporting accounts: Conflicting expedition maps and repeated descriptions of shifting landmarks.

Challenges: The maps may be inaccurate copies or deliberate fabrications.

Next question: Can independent expeditions reproduce the same changes?

THEORY / TH-003 / UNCONFIRMED

The Resonant Boundary

Proposal: Certain repeated sound patterns may correspond with unusual changes near Veil boundaries.

Supporting accounts: Expedition notes describing similar tones before reported spatial disturbances.

Challenges: No reliable causal connection has been demonstrated.

Next question: Are the sounds a cause, a consequence, or coincidence?

Researcher's Reminder

A theory must be allowed to fail. New evidence may strengthen it, weaken it, or reveal that the original question was wrong.

Write down what you think. Then write down what would prove you wrong.
