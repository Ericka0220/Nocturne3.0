CUSTOMS, PATTERNS, AND REPORTED EFFECTS

Rituals

A collection of fictional ritual accounts preserved for historical and narrative study.

Important Research Notice

Ritual entries in this archive are fictional worldbuilding records. They are not real instructions, verified supernatural practices, or recommendations for real-world activity. The archive focuses on narrative origins, reported effects, symbolism, and uncertainty.

RITUAL / R-001 / UNCONFIRMED

The Lantern Vigil

Origin: The northern settlements of the Known World.

Account: A winter tradition describing a lantern left burning in an empty window. Local stories claim the light can guide a lost traveler toward familiar ground.

Reported effect: Witnesses describe seeing a second light appear in the distance.

Research note: No consistent explanation has been established. Weather, distance, and local storytelling may account for parts of the tradition.

RITUAL / R-002 / LEGENDARY

The Hollow Bell

Origin: An abandoned monastery recorded in regional folklore.

Account: A bell said to ring once when someone speaks a name that has been erased from the monastery's records.

Reported effect: A single tone heard from an otherwise silent tower.

Research note: The bell itself has not been located. Surviving accounts disagree about the name and the building's location.

RITUAL / R-003 / DISPUTED

The Ashen Index

Origin: An anonymous manuscript recovered from a private collection.

Account: A symbolic ceremony said to reveal a forgotten entry in an archive.

Reported effect: A page appears to contain writing that was not visible before.

Research note: The manuscript's age and provenance are disputed. The account may be an allegory about memory and loss.

Recording a Ritual

Researchers should document the claimed origin, cultural context, source of the account, reported effect, inconsistencies, and confidence level. Missing information should remain marked as missing.
