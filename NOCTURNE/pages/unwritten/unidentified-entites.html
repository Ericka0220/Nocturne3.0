SOMETHING WAS THERE

Unidentified Entities

Reports of beings that have not been named, classified, or adequately understood.

Identification Is Not Assumption

An entity is placed in this collection when available evidence does not support a reliable identification. Descriptions may be incomplete, contradictory, or based on a single witness.

Witness descriptions are preserved separately from researcher interpretations. Similar appearances do not necessarily indicate the same being.

ENTITY / UE-001 / UNCONFIRMED

The Antlered Figure

Appearance: A tall silhouette with branching structures resembling antlers.

Reported location: Woodland borders near the northern settlements.

Behavior: Described as remaining at a distance and disappearing behind trees.

Evidence: Three witness accounts. No verified physical trace.

Research question: Do the accounts describe one entity or several unrelated encounters?

ENTITY / UE-002 / DISPUTED

The Window Watcher

Appearance: A pale figure reportedly visible through windows in abandoned buildings.

Reported location: Multiple disused properties.

Behavior: Witnesses describe the figure facing inward rather than outward.

Evidence: Conflicting accounts and one unverified photograph.

Research question: Could the reports be connected to reflections or other visual distortions?

ENTITY / UE-003 / UNRESOLVED

The Ashen Child

Appearance: A small figure described as covered in grey dust or ash.

Reported location: An abandoned settlement near the Veil.

Behavior: Accounts describe the figure standing near doorways before vanishing.

Evidence: A collection of oral accounts with no independent confirmation.

Research question: Why do the descriptions repeatedly mention the same unusual details?

ENTITY / UE-004 / UNIDENTIFIED

The Sound Beneath the Lake

Appearance: No visual description available.

Reported location: A deep lake in the eastern region.

Behavior: A low sound reportedly heard beneath the water during still nights.

Evidence: Two expedition journals and an incomplete recording.

Research question: Is the source biological, geological, mechanical, or something else?

Unidentified Entity Checklist

  • Preserve original witness descriptions.
  • Record the time and location of every report.
  • Compare accounts without forcing similarities.
  • Document missing or contradictory evidence.
  • Do not assign a species or identity prematurely.

The first question is not what it is. The first question is what was actually seen.
