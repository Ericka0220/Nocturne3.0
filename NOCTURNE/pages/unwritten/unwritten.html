What Is The Unwritten?

The Unwritten is NOCTURNE's collection of incomplete knowledge. It holds unidentified entities, missing records, lost locations, unresolved investigations, and questions that have resisted explanation.

It is not a collection of failures. It is a record of the boundaries of current understanding.

Some entries may eventually be resolved. Others may be reclassified, disproven, or connected to discoveries that have not yet been made.

Rules of the Unwritten

  • Unknown does not automatically mean supernatural.
  • Missing evidence must never be replaced with invented certainty.
  • Contradictory accounts should be preserved.
  • Every unresolved entry may be revisited when new information appears.
  • A case may remain open indefinitely.

Leave a little room at the end of the page. Something may belong there later.
