Active Case Register

CASE / OI-001 / ACTIVE

The Hollowing Woods

Department: Veil Research and Cartography Division.

Known evidence: Conflicting route maps, missing time reports, and unusual environmental observations.

Primary question: Why do separate expeditions describe different routes through the same forest?

Next action: Compare historical maps and independent field notes.

CASE / OI-002 / ACTIVE

The Unnamed Signal

Department: Observatory Division.

Known evidence: Several incomplete recordings of a repeated signal from an unidentified source.

Primary question: Is the signal environmental, mechanical, or connected to a celestial event?

Next action: Locate the original recording equipment and compare timestamps.

CASE / OI-003 / PENDING REVIEW

The Seventh Register

Department: Archive Division.

Known evidence: References to a missing volume in surviving catalogue records.

Primary question: Was the volume ever completed?

Next action: Search historical correspondence and inventory records.

CASE / OI-004 / ACTIVE

The Winter Key

Department: Preservation Division and Grimoire researchers.

Known evidence: An iron key with unusual temperature behavior and no identified lock.

Primary question: What is the object's origin and why does it remain cold?

Next action: Continue provenance research and non-invasive examination.
