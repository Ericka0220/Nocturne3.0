Geographical Overview

The NOCTURNE world is organized into three broad layers of geographical and dimensional understanding.

I. The Known World

Ordinary geography, documented settlements, historical territories, and established locations.

II. The Veil

Regions associated with unusual environmental conditions, spatial inconsistencies, and reported crossings.

III. The Beyond

Territories described in accounts that do not fit established geographical models.

Map Legend

  • Solid lines: Documented boundaries.
  • Dashed lines: Approximate or disputed boundaries.
  • Red markings: Locations requiring further investigation.
  • Blank regions: Unmapped or unavailable information.
  • Crossed-out labels: Historical names no longer in use.

Cartographer's Note

The blank spaces are not mistakes. They are reminders of how much remains unknown.
