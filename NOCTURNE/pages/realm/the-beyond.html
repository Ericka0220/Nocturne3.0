The Beyond

What lies outside the edges of our understanding?

Beyond Established Geography

The Beyond is a name used within NOCTURNE to describe territories and environments that cannot currently be placed within the established geographical structure of the Known World.

It is not a confirmed destination or a single mapped region. Its nature remains uncertain.
