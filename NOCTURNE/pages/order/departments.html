Departments

The divisions responsible for maintaining the network.

Department Directory

NOCTURNE is organized into specialized divisions. Each department has its own responsibilities, research methods, and records.

Central Directorate

Network coordination, internal administration, and oversight.

Archive Division

Document preservation, record management, and historical research.

Veil Research

Investigation of unusual phenomena and reports associated with the Veil.

Cartography Division

Geographical research, mapping, and expedition documentation.

Cultural Division

Folklore, mythology, cultural history, and oral traditions.

Observatory Division

Celestial records, environmental observations, and temporal investigations.

Preservation Division

Physical collections, recovered objects, and conservation.

Departmental Independence

No department possesses complete knowledge of the network. Research findings are shared, challenged, and revised across divisions.
