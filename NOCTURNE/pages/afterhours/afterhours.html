The Sound Archive

Afterhours is the audio collection of NOCTURNE. It preserves music, environmental recordings, unusual transmissions, recovered audio, and soundscapes connected to the network.

Some recordings are artistic interpretations. Others are reconstructed from incomplete accounts. A few have no confirmed source.
