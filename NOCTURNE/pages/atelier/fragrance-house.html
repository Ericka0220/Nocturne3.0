The Fragrance House

Every memory has a scent. Every mystery leaves an impression.

Olfactory Studies

The Fragrance House is a conceptual collection of scents inspired by landscapes, memories, historical periods, and fictional places within the NOCTURNE universe.

Fragrance descriptions are creative concepts, not claims about supernatural properties.

FRAGRANCE 001 · CONCEPT

Velvet Nocturne

Family: Amber floral

Top Notes: Blackcurrant, bergamot

Heart Notes: Dark rose, violet

Base Notes: Amber, cedar, musk

A deep floral composition inspired by candlelit rooms, dark velvet, and old libraries.

FRAGRANCE 002 · CONCEPT

Hollow Woods

Family: Woody aromatic

Top Notes: Pine, juniper

Heart Notes: Moss, cedar

Base Notes: Vetiver, damp earth

A woodland-inspired composition with the atmosphere of a forest after rainfall.

FRAGRANCE 003 · CONCEPT

Crimson Reliquary

Family: Spiced floral

Top Notes: Pink pepper, plum

Heart Notes: Rose, incense

Base Notes: Patchouli, dark woods

A rich composition inspired by antique reliquaries, velvet-lined boxes, and forgotten ceremonial objects.
