The Atelier

Where imagination becomes something worth preserving.

The Collection House

The Atelier is the creative and artistic division of NOCTURNE. It preserves visual works, extraordinary objects, crafted collections, and creative experiments.

Some works are created by network contributors. Others are recovered from unusual locations, commissioned for exhibitions, or reconstructed from incomplete historical descriptions.
