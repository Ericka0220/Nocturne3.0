Creative Direction

Velvet Nocturne explores the relationship between scent, memory, and atmosphere. Its visual identity draws inspiration from antique perfume bottles, dark floral arrangements, velvet-lined boxes, and handwritten labels.

Visual Identity

Primary Color: Deep Burgundy

Secondary Color: Antique Gold

Accent Color: Aged Ivory

Typography: Editorial serif with handwritten details

Materials: Glass, textured paper, metallic foil
