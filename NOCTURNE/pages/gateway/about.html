About NOCTURNE

Understanding the network behind the records.

What Is NOCTURNE?

NOCTURNE is an independent research and preservation network dedicated to the exploration of extraordinary phenomena, forgotten histories, unusual locations, mysterious objects, and the stories surrounding them.

We do not claim to understand everything we document. We collect accounts, investigate evidence, preserve information, and acknowledge what remains unknown.

Our Purpose

  • Document unexplained phenomena.
  • Preserve folklore, myths, and forgotten histories.
  • Investigate unusual creatures and reported encounters.
  • Catalog extraordinary objects and locations.
  • Connect discoveries across different disciplines.
  • Encourage curiosity, research, and creative exploration.

Our Philosophy

A story is not proof of its own existence. But a story is still worth preserving.

Every record is open to examination. Every theory may be challenged. Every discovery may lead to something we have not yet considered.

The Three Layers

The Known World

The world of ordinary geography, history, and experience.

The Veil

The boundary where unusual phenomena and ordinary reality intersect.

The Beyond

What may exist outside the limits of our current understanding.
